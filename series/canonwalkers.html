Episode 1: Taszo and Friends

The sun finally rises on our heroes at the beginning of their journey. A Shin man brings the Canonwalkers the Opportunity to search for a lost artifact, yet they ultimately find themselves at a crossroads. Are the old legends true or are they simply the ravings of a madman?

Episode 2: Burying the Lead

Opportunity has blessed our heroes to walk away from their first combat relatively unscathed, but the same cannot be said about those left at the Crossroads. Join the Canonwalkers in today’s episode as they journey into the stormwall and whatever may await them.

Episode 3: The Chouta Club

The Canonwalkers finally make it to the warcamps. They’re ready to begin their search for Taszo’s mysterious contact, but the appearance of a group of mysterious figures gives our heroes an Opportunity to search the chasms. What secrets await them, and where did Nmra obtain so many chouta rolls?

Nmra

Player: Krishna | Path: Envoy/Hunter | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: She/Her

Nmra is a Thaylen merchant apprentice under the mysterious Babsk, who found her selling fish in Klna. With a passion for discovery, she documents everything in her notebook—from Alethi fashion to Unkalaki cuisine. Despite her knowledge of the world, Nmra's true drive is uncovering the secrets of Roshar. Her journey begins with her missing brother, Tmlakyn, who disappeared years ago after leaving with his own babsk. No one's heard from him since, but Nmra is determined to follow any lead she can find. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Tylin

Player: Tyler | Path: Scholar/Truthwatcher | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: He/Him

Tylin, born in Kasitor to immigrant Reshi and Shin parents, grew up without the golden features typical of Iri natives. After two years in the Kasitor City Watch, he sought adventure by joining a merchant caravan bound for Azir. The journey ended in tragedy when bandits attacked, leading to his enslavement and a brutal march across Roshar. Escaping near Kholinar without any resources or connections, Tylin suvived by taking any mercenary work available. His latest job finds him guarding a Kholin caravan heading for the Shattered Plains—escorting a strange and secretive prisoner. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Vardah

Player: Sean | Path: Agent/Stoneward | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: He/Him

Vardah has never been one to sit still. A deep desire to explore led him at a very young age to seek out new opportunities and expand his horizons (both metaphorically and literally), resulting in his arrival at the Alethi warcamps. Burdened with both skill and a desire to be useful, Vardah finds himself surrounded by people he doesn't understand, serving as a scout to those who pursue objectives he doesn't agree with. But that won't stop him from chasing his own goals. Hopefully, in turn, he can achieve the one thing he truly cares about: finding peace for his family. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Spark

Player: Charlotte | Path: Warrior/Stoneward | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: She/Her

Spark is a towering Horneater and mountain guide. She served as a guide for a group bound for the Shattered Plains, leaving behind her little brother who she loves fiercely and would fight the mountain itself to protect. She now serves in Sebarial's warcamp where her quick fists and quicker temper earned her both respect and trouble. After an "incident" with a lighteyed officer, Sebarial—who was amused but pragmatic—assigned her to an expedition outside the Shattered Plains to give everyone a little space to let bruised egos heal and memories grow fuzzy. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Farav

Player: ReaderWarrior | Path: Warrior/Scholar/Edgedancer | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: He/Him

Farav, proud and disciplined, dreamed of becoming a soldier after witnessing the Blackthorn's march. He trained for years, only to lose his leg in an accident caused by Brightlord Marad before ever seeing battle on the Shattered Plains. Now a blacksmith, he forged his own prosthetic and a new life with his wife, unborn child, and apprentice, Torvin. Yet despite his new purpose, Farav still gazes toward the battlefield, longing for the honor and glory that slipped through his fingers. (Art by Madi VanDoren)
