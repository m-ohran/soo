Episode 1: Prologue

While there are those who follow a set story, there are those who journey to create their own destination. Follow along as Opportunity invests itself within new arrivals and residents of the war camps and propels them forward to adventure within the Unclaimed Hills. Enjoy the first episode of the Loose Canons!

Episode 2: New Opportunities

A window of Opportunity opens for Tevlin and Grove to slip away under the cover of night, but Batarab and Nakal are close behind. The Loose Canons set off toward their destination, but no true journey is one where everything is planned.

Episode 3: Those Who Could Save

After a long trek through the Unclaimed Hills, the Loose Canons find themselves at an ancient ruin. It reeks of death and betrayal - and an Opportunity for justice to be brought forward. Maybe it lies in treasure or, perhaps, another step forward in the path untrodden. Tune in once again for the next adventure of the Loose Canons homebrew adventure!

Characters

Player Characters

Batarab Cadilar

Player: Cate | Path: Leader/Lightweaver | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: She/Her

Batarab Cadilar, an Alethi lighteyes born to a Companylord and a battle-axe of a mother, has been raised to embody all feminine Vorin values. Inspired by Jasnah's claim that a woman should choose their own role—what role does she truly want? Batarab loves to write and record but struggles to distinguish the rest of her true self from her upbringing. Perhaps she shouldn't be so horrified that her cousin Tevlin has dragged her on an adventure—societal pressure means little when you might become whitespine food. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Tevlin Hasham

Player: Joshua (Jofwu) | Path: Scholar/Willshaper | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: She/Her

Tevlin is a young lighteyed woman from Jah Keved with big dreams of becoming a world famous archeologist. She’s a scholar who wants to be outside getting crem on her hands rather than stuck inside a dusty old library. Tevlin is an extrovert, full of energy and a hunger for new adventures. She likes to challenge boundaries and ideas...But doesn't always think through the consequences or have the patience to reign back her often-hasty impulses. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Grove

Player: Emily | Path: Hunter | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: She/Her

Grove is a lighthearted and genuine Horneater who is quick to make friends. She admires the Kaluk’i’iki, the elite guides for the gods, and desires to join their ranks one day. Encouraged by her Wayfarer mother to travel Roshar and improve her hunting and tracking skills, she ended up in the Shattered Plains, eager to prove she has what it takes to pursue her dreams. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Nakal Palar

Player: Doug | Path: Warrior/Stoneward | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: He/Him

Nakal Palar is a life-long soldier like his father before him. His dedication to his duty is one of his driving factors and his instilled care for protecting the innocent stems from a lifelong of serving as a house guard. After some "disagreements" with the light eyes of his house about the use of slaves he was "promoted" to Batarab's personal guard. (Art by Madi VanDoren)

Bryn

Player: Chris (Doc) | Path: Scholar/Edgedancer | Ancestry: Human | Pronouns: He/Him

Bryn is an Iriali medic trained in Kharbranth. He has a very fact driven mind and until recently had not spent a lot of time around people. As a devout follower of the One, he tends to believe that all experiences, even bad ones, are valuable and important. Yet after traveling to the Shattered Planes and spending time with Listener groups willing to accept him, he has begun to see the atrocities of war first hand. The pain and suffering are starting to cause cracks in his faith. He records his own experiences and those of all the people he meets. They must be remembered. (Art by Madi VanDoren)
